Think deeply about this problem before answering—skip the quick, surface-level solution.

1. Frame the problem in its full context and surface hidden constraints or assumptions.
2. Examine every relevant dimension: correctness, edge cases, performance, maintainability, failure modes, risks, and reversibility.
3. Compare serious alternatives and their real tradeoffs.
4. Propose a well-reasoned recommendation explaining why it beats the alternatives.
